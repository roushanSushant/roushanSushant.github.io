How this works: Edit content here, then click Download updated portfolio-data.js. Upload that downloaded file to your GitHub repository and replace the old one. This static website cannot save changes directly to GitHub.

Adding photos and documents

  1. In your GitHub repository, create or open assets/photos/ and upload the image.
  2. In this editor, enter its path in the image field, for example assets/photos/field-visit-01.jpg.
  3. For your CV, upload a PDF into assets/ and set the CV field in Profile & contact to assets/cv.pdf.
  4. Keep file names short and avoid spaces. Only upload photos and documents you have permission to publish.

Good practice

  • For each project, add your exact role, dates, outputs, evidence and lessons learned.
  • For testimonials, get permission to publish the person's name, role and quote.
  • Do not publish confidential government documents, beneficiary information or unapproved photographs.
  • After uploading changes, refresh your public website. GitHub Pages may take a short time to update.
