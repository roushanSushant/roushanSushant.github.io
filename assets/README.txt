# Portfolio assets

Place approved public-facing files here.

- `photos/`: field photographs, project images and profile photo
- `documents/`: public reports, policy briefs and writing samples
- `cv.pdf`: optional CV, placed directly in `assets/`

Upload assets to GitHub and reference them with relative paths such as `assets/photos/field-visit-01.jpg`. Obtain consent before publishing photos of identifiable people and permission before publishing testimonials or documents. Do not upload confidential government records or personal information about beneficiaries.
